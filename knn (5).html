Learning notes

RGB = 3×8 bit = 24 bit/pixel (conceptual raw; browsers store RGBA). Gray = 1×8 bit: hue/saturation lost. Binary = 1 bit: all intensity detail lost except above/below threshold. Blur averages neighbours (noise ↓, detail ↓). Sobel measures intensity gradient √(Gx²+Gy²). KNN predicts by majority vote of the K closest labelled points in feature space.
